Grade 12 Students Excel in 2025 ECZ National Examinations

Parklands Secondary School is proud to announce outstanding results in the 2025 Examinations Council of Zambia (ECZ) national examinations. With an overall pass rate of 95%, 12 students achieved distinctions across all subjects — the school's best result in its history.

Our Science stream led the way, with 6 distinctions, followed closely by Commerce with 4. The school ranked 3rd in Lusaka Province, a testament to the dedication of our students, teachers, and parents.
