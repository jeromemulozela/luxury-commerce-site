Our Heritage

A Decade of Shaping Tomorrow's Leaders

Parklands Secondary School was founded with a singular conviction: that every young person, given the right environment, can achieve extraordinary things.

Over the years, we have grown from a small community school into one of Lusaka's most respected academic institutions — without ever losing sight of the values that define us.

Our motto, "Narrow path is the way," speaks to a timeless truth: meaningful achievement requires discipline, perseverance, and the courage to do what is right — not what is easy.
