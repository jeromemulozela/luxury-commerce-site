Eligibility

Admission Requirements

📋 Grade 8 Entry

  • Grade 7 results slip (Primary School Leaving Certificate)
  • Minimum 50% overall average in Grade 7
  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Completed application form
  • Non-refundable application fee: ZMW 150

📋 Transfer Students (Gr 9–11)

  • Latest school report / academic transcript
  • Transfer letter from previous school
  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Completed application form
  • Non-refundable application fee: ZMW 150
